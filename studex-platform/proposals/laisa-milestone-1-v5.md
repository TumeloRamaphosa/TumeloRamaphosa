## Executive Summary

LAISA is an autonomous clinic operating system powered by StudEx Ai OS — a unified platform that automates administrative burden, coordinates clinical workflows, and enables data-driven decision-making for healthcare providers.

**The Problem:**
- Doctors spend **50% of their time on paperwork** (insurance claims, prior authorizations, clinical notes)
- This represents **~30% of clinic operational costs**
- McKinsey identifies medical administration as a **top-3 AI value pool** for healthcare

**The Solution:**
LAISA combines three layers:
1. **Autonomous Agent Team** — handles scheduling, compliance, reporting, content
2. **Unified Dashboard** — KPI tracking, staff coordination, real-time operations visibility
3. **StudEx Cognitive Brain** — memory layer recording every agent action and staff decision for compliance, optimization, and institutional knowledge

**The Result:**
Clinic recovers ~30% operational overhead. Doctors focus on patient care. Compliance becomes automatic.

**C. StudEx Cognitive Brain (Preview)**
- Day-by-day record of every agent action and staff decision
- Searchable by category (admin, clinical, compliance, content)

**D. Skill Marketplace (Preview)**
- 40+ agent skills visible in one interface

- One-click activation (no coding, no setup)
- ROI metrics per skill (e.g., "This skill recovers 4 hours/week per doctor")

**E. White-Label Branding**
- All marketing and dashboards branded as "LAISA" (StudEx tech invisible to end users)
