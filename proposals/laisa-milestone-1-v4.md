### Phase 2 (sections 3.1–3.5) — unchanged from v3

### 3.6 Lausanne Medical Supplements — your subscription store (Phase 3)
Six doctor-curated products (Collagen, Vitamin D3, Omega-3, NAD+,
Longevity Stack, Weight Support). Conservative Year-1 projection R1.44M
new revenue. Year-2 R10.8M ARR.

### 3.7 StudEx Cognitive Brain — your living memory (Phase 3) — NEW

Every patient interaction, every agent action, every content decision
lands in the brain automatically. The brain is the memory layer that
binds every agent and every staff member.

- **Categorised** — Patients, Marketing, Clinical, Comms, Content, Store, Leadership, Intel
- **Searchable** — "Mrs Khoza follow-up", "LASIK conversions", "Lausanne NAD+ renewals"
- **Day-by-day** — click any date, see the whole operation that day
- **Read + write by every agent and every staff member**
- **Indexed knowledge graph** under the hood — long-term recall, fast retrieval
- **Voice notes from doctors** auto-transcribed and written to the brain
- **Source of truth for the agents** — they read here before they act, write here after they act

This is the difference between "a dashboard with a chatbot" and "a
clinic that knows itself."

### 3.8 Staff app + dashboard (Phase 3) — NEW

Every team member (Q5: 5 doctors, 3 admin, 6 support) uses the StudEx
app on iPad on the clinic floor.

- **Reception** logs check-ins, books bookings, hands off to assistants
- **Ophthalmic assistants** log scans, route patients to doctors
- **Doctors** log consults, dictate notes (voice → brain), schedule follow-ups
- **Aesthetics therapists** log treatments, product recommendations
- **Theatre support** logs surgical setup + turnover

Every log entry is auto-categorised and written to the brain. The
dashboard shows the same data, real-time, to management. The wall
display in §3.9 below is the same data again, on the wall.

**One source of truth. Every device a window into it.**

### 3.9 Live ops wall (Phase 3) — NEW

Office displays show what is happening right now. **Public mode** runs
on front-of-house screens — bookings today, channel reach, patient
satisfaction. **Private mode** runs on back-office screens — live MRR,
agent chats stream, full revenue.

- Five live tickers (bookings, messages routed, posts published, orders shipped, MRR)
- "What is happening right now" stream — every agent + staff action as it occurs
- Two modes, same feed, role-aware visibility

You walk through the practice and see the business breathing.

### 3.10 Mobile app — iOS + Android (Phase 3) — NEW

Three role-aware experiences, one codebase:

- **Patient app** — book 24/7, chat with Charlie, buy Lausanne
  Supplements, see treatment plan + recovery progress, Face-ID gated
  records
- **Doctor app** — today + week calendar, patient summaries, voice notes
  auto-written to brain, two-tap referrals
- **Staff iPad app** — log activity as you work, reception check-in flow,
  hand-offs, real-time business view per role

Everything connects to the dashboard. Everything writes to the brain.

### 3.11 POPIA-aligned local hub (Phase 4) — unchanged from v3

Detail in v3 §3.7. The answer to PRD Q40 (your AI/integration/data-quality
concern) — patient data stays in South Africa.
