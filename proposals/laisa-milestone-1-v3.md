## 3. The Seven New Capabilities — wireframed today, built in later phases

### 3.1 Agent operations — your back-of-house team (Phase 2)

Six specialist agents working in coordination, with a defined day
schedule. Humans approve, agents execute.

| Agent | Job |
|-------|-----|
| Competitor Scout | Watches 27 competitor accounts hourly |
| Content Composer | Drafts the day's posts from competitor signal + brand voice |
| Engagement Manager | Replies to comments and DMs across every channel |
| Inbox Triage | Routes incoming messages, books appointments, escalates clinical |
| Insights Analyst | Builds the morning leadership brief |
| Store Ops | Reconciles orders and ships subscription deliveries |

**A day in the orchestra:**

| Time | Who | What |
|------|-----|------|
| 06:00 | Inbox Triage | Summarises overnight messages across every channel |
| 07:00 | Competitor Scout | Pulls top-5 accounts' last 24h posts + comments + engagement |
| 08:00 | Insights Analyst | Builds the shareholders' morning brief |
| 09:00 | Leadership (you) | Daily brief delivered — humans review and decide |
| 10:00 | Content Composer | Drafts the day's 5 posts |
| 11:00 | Human approver | Reviews drafted posts in the approval queue |
| 13:00 | Publishing Agent | Publishes approved posts across IG, FB, LinkedIn, TikTok, X |
| 14:00 | Engagement Manager | Replies to comments and DMs as they arrive |
| 16:00 | Store Ops | Reconciles orders, dispatches subscription deliveries |
| 18:00 | Insights Analyst | End-of-day summary + tomorrow's prep |

### 3.2 Competitor intelligence (Phase 2)
Top-5 accounts in your category watched continuously. Posts, comments,
formats and engagement patterns mined every hour and rolled into the
morning brief. Replaces the marketing-consultant fees you would otherwise
pay (~R15K–R25K / month) and produces sharper creative direction.

### 3.3 Content pipeline — 5 posts a day (Phase 2)
Agent drafts → human approves → Publishing Agent ships across IG, FB,
LinkedIn, TikTok, X. **5× your current cadence** with no extra
marketing-officer time.

### 3.4 Unified inbox — every channel, one queue (Phase 2)
WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, email and website chat all
land in one place. Charlie triages, books appointments, escalates clinical
to humans. **24/7 lead capture replaces business-hours-only reception.**

### 3.5 Doctor calendars — coordinated booking surface (Phase 2)
Each doctor's day on the dashboard, the week on each doctor's page.
Shared booking surface so the agent, reception and doctors never collide.
**Eliminates the double-booking and missed-slot losses you carry today.**

### 3.6 Lausanne Medical Supplements — your subscription store (Phase 3)
Your own doctor-curated supplement line — Collagen, Vitamin D3, Omega-3,
NAD+, Longevity Stack, Weight Support — on monthly subscription. Storefront
connected to the same dashboard. Orders flow into Store Ops automatically.

**Conservative Year-1 projection** (200 subscribers × R600 avg):
**R1.44M new annual revenue** at near-zero marginal effort once the
agent team is running. Year-2 target of 1,500 subscribers takes that to
**R10.8M ARR**.

### 3.7 Local hub — your data, your premises (Phase 4)
An on-premise orchestration appliance sits in your reception. Patient
records, clinical notes, scans and medical-aid claims **stay in South
Africa**. Only anonymised metrics ride to the dashboard. AES-256 at rest,
TLS 1.3 in flight. Four access tiers (Doctor, Admin, Reception, Patient).

**This is your answer to Q40 ("AI risk, integration risk, patient-care
integrity") — you keep sovereignty over your data while still getting the
intelligence benefit.**
