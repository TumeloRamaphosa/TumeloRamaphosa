# OR Tambo International Airport

Johannesburg's main international airport (IATA **JNB**). The airport the
[[aviar-vip]] VIP escort is built around. Approx. centre: -26.133, 28.242.

The Aviar VIP walking track runs from VIP arrivals at Terminal A (International
Departures) through baggage, fast-track security, the Premier Lounge, the
concourse walk, and a final apron transfer to the aircraft — each waypoint
previewable in Google Street View.
