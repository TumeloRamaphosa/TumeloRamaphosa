# StudExMeat — Instagram Launch Kit (Higgsfield)

A 7-post launch pack for `@studexmeat`. Every post has a Higgsfield prompt
(Soul for the still, DoP/Speak for the motion), a caption, hashtags, and a
music cue. Paste prompts directly into the Higgsfield UI.

---

## 1. Brand Voice

**One line:** Premium African meat, traced from soil to plate by agentic AI.

**Tone:** Bold. Cinematic. Pan-African. Founder-energy meets fine-dining.
Confident, never corporate. Speaks farmer and chef in the same sentence.

**Voice rules**
- Short sentences. Active verbs.
- Concrete over abstract — say "Karoo lamb at 14 months" not "quality meat".
- Name the place: Karoo, Limpopo, Eastern Cape, Free State.
- Tech is invisible. We don't say "AI-powered traceability" — we say
  "scan the QR. Meet the farmer who raised it."
- No emojis in body copy. One, max, at the end of a caption.
- Never "we are excited to announce". Just announce.

**Words we use:** dry-aged, grass-fed, pasture, herd, butchery, terroir,
provenance, cut, marbling, age, grade, biltong, braai.

**Words we don't:** synergy, leverage, world-class, game-changing, journey
(unless literal), unlock, ecosystem.

---

## 2. Higgsfield Setup

| Need | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero stills (cattle, cuts, farms) | **Soul** | Photoreal product + landscape |
| Cinematic motion on a still | **DoP** | Camera moves, depth, dolly |
| Talking-head reels (founder, chef) | **Speak** | Lip-sync from a still |
| Stylised brand moments | **Steal** | Reference-locked aesthetic |

**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 for reels, 4:5 for feed carousels.
**Length:** 5–10s clips, stitch 2–3 in CapCut for full reels.
