(Buy Now, Pay Later at checkout) + 3 stories + a 6-second launch reel.

> **Accuracy note before posting:** Stitch Express is a unified checkout that

## 5. Brand Voice Reminders (don't drift on this post)

- Never "we're excited to announce". Just announce.
- Say the number. R1,200 → 4 × R300. Specifics outsell adjectives.
- Mention the meat by name (Karoo lamb, dry-aged ribeye), not "products".
- Stitch is the partner, not the hero. Hero is the meat.
- One emoji max in the caption. Zero in the overlay text.
