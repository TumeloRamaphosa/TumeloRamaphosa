# Process — Customer Follow-up

1. **Trigger**: new order, abandoned cart, delivery done, or a win-back window.
2. **Draft** (`sales-cx`) → `/customer-followup` → 2-3 variants, POPIA-safe, opt-out included.
3. **Approve** (owner) → review in mission-control.
4. **Send** → via the approved channel (email / WhatsApp / DM). Never before approval.
5. **Log** → outcome noted; retention learnings promoted to `wiki/`.
