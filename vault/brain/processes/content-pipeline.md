# Process — Content Pipeline (one idea → everywhere)

The repeatable flow the crew runs for a content sprint.

1. **Research** (`researcher`) → topic/keyword + angle. `/research-job` if deep.
2. **Article** (`marketer`) → `/seo-article` → `vault/outputs/content/articles/`.
3. **Atomise** (`social`) → `/social-pipeline` turns the article into a week of posts.
4. **Video** (`social`) → `/video-pipeline` turns the best hooks into short videos.
5. **Approve** (owner) → review the queue in mission-control (Decisions/Field-Ops).
6. **Publish** → scheduled to channels (only after approval).
7. **Measure** (`analyst`) → `/growth-report` reads what performed.
8. **Promote** (`chief-of-staff`) → `/vault-cleanup` moves winners to `wiki/`.
